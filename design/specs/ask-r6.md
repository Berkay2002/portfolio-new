# Ask r6: large and centre, combined

Status: superseded by ask-r7 (2026-10-06).

Berkay on `ask-r4`: a combination of a-large and c-centre; b-signal and d-terminal are bad. From `ask-r5`'s
note: no example questions, no status line, rounded like ChatGPT's and Claude's composer, with a hint of
the trace. Two takes on the mix: centred (c's layout with a's big composer) and left (a's layout with c's
softer composer).

References attached with -i, in this order: the approved landing hero (`design/approved/landing-desktop.png`),
the approved phone boards (`design/approved/landing-mobile-1.png`, the first phone only), `ask-r4-a-large`
and `ask-r4-c-centre` (`design/mockups/`). Keep the portrait exactly as it is there; it is a real photo
and must not be redrawn.

## Shared

A clean presentation board, 1536x1024, flat deep graphite #0A0B0C. On the left, about 1080 px wide, a
flat screenshot of the first screen of a desktop website, starting with its header (no browser chrome).
On the right, one iPhone-style screen (390x844 proportions, thin dark bezel, rounded corners), straight
on, no perspective, no hands, showing the first screen on a phone. Even gaps, nothing else on the board,
no watermark.

The website is Berkay Orhan's portfolio in the look of the attached images: the same header ("Berkay
Orhan" left; "Work", "Research", "Experience", "About", "EN / SV" in small monospace; a lime "Contact"
button) and the same portrait of him in the black hoodie fading into the graphite. The hero holds only
three things: the headline "I build AI that" / "works outside the demo.", under it the lede in dim
monospace, "Agentic retrieval, voice pipelines and the web apps around them. Now a software developer at
Ericsson.", and the composer. Nothing else: no overline, no buttons, no example questions, no status
line, no hints, no labels.

The composer is the heart of the screen, shaped like the message composer of ChatGPT or Claude: a large,
generously rounded field, filled a touch lighter than the page (#17191C) with a very thin #2A2D31
outline, the placeholder "Ask me anything about my work" in off-white grotesk at about 22 px at its top
left with a thin lime caret before it, and a round lime send button (about 44 px) at its bottom right with
a graphite up arrow drawn in two strokes. Room for a second line of text. Calm, soft and simple. On the
desktop it is empty and focused. On the phone it is full width, and the visitor has asked "Which projects
use LangGraph?": the question stays in the composer, and under it, with no label, the answer in
off-white grotesk at about 16 px: "Two of them. The Municipality Chatbot uses LangGraph to orchestrate
its answers, and SynGraph uses it to run a supervisor and its worker agents.", with "Municipality
Chatbot" and "SynGraph" lit lime.

The hint of the trace: the hero's thin lime waveform runs across under the composer as in the references,
flat with a burst, and that is the only lime besides the caret, the send button and the Contact button.

Palette: background deep graphite #0F1012, text off-white #ECEAE4, dim text #8A8C90, faint #2A2D31, one
accent acid lime #C8F542. No other hues, no gradients, no glow, no shadows, no 3D. Type: headlines in a
geometric grotesk like Space Grotesk 600 with tight tracking; the lede in a monospace like JetBrains
Mono. All text in English, crisp and legible, exactly as given here.

Must not appear: chat bubbles, avatars, robot or sparkle icons, microphone or paperclip icons, emoji,
logos, chips or pills of text, blue links, gradients, glow, any other person, any change to his face,
any text not written here.

## a-centred

The layout of `ask-r4-c-centre`: the portrait large and dim behind, centred above; the headline centred
at about 64 px; the lede centred under it; and centred under them the composer of `ask-r4-a-large`, wide
(about 820 px) and tall (about 128 px), corner radius about 28 px. The waveform runs across the bottom
of the screen. On the phone, everything centred and stacked the same way.

## b-left

The layout of `ask-r4-a-large`: the headline and the lede at the top left, the portrait on the right as
in the reference. Under them, a large composer about 760 px wide and 128 px tall, corner radius about
28 px, running a little over the portrait's shoulder. The waveform runs across under it. On the phone,
left-aligned and stacked.
