# Ask r9: the original hero, with an Ask button that opens the composer

Status: built (2026-10-06); the conversation it opens into is ask-r8 a-bubbles.

Berkay on the built `ask-r7` hero and the `ask-r8` a-bubbles conversation: keep the original hero from
main (overline, headline, lede, "See my work" and "Download CV"), and add a button that opens the
composer; a question sent from it turns the hero into the a-bubbles conversation. Two frames of the
flow: the hero at rest with the button, and the hero once the button is pressed. References attached
with -i, in this order: the approved hero `design/approved/landing-desktop.png`, the approved phones
`design/approved/landing-mobile-1.png` (the first phone only) and the composer
`design/approved/landing-hero-ask.png`. Keep the portrait exactly as it is in them; it is a real photo
and must not be redrawn.

## Shared

A clean presentation board, 1536x1024, flat deep graphite #0A0B0C. On the left, about 1080 px wide, a
flat screenshot of the first screen of a desktop website, starting with its header (no browser chrome).
On the right, one iPhone-style screen (390x844 proportions, thin dark bezel, rounded corners), straight
on, no perspective, no hands, showing the first screen on a phone. Even gaps, nothing else on the board,
no watermark.

The website is Berkay Orhan's portfolio exactly as in the first two attached images: the same header
("Berkay Orhan" left; "Work", "Research", "Experience", "About", "Ask", "EN / SV" in small monospace; a
lime "Contact" button), the same portrait of him in the black hoodie on the right of the desktop and
above the text on the phone, the overline "SOFTWARE DEVELOPER · LINKÖPING" in small lime monospace, the
headline "I build AI that" / "works outside the demo." and the lede "Agentic retrieval, voice pipelines
and the web apps around them. Now a software developer at Ericsson." in dim monospace, and the thin lime
waveform running across the bottom of the hero, flat with a burst.

Palette: background deep graphite #0F1012, text off-white #ECEAE4, dim text #8A8C90, faint #2A2D31, one
accent acid lime #C8F542. No other hues, no gradients, no glow, no shadows, no 3D. Type: headlines in a
geometric grotesk like Space Grotesk 600 with tight tracking; the rest in a monospace like JetBrains
Mono. All text in English, crisp and legible, exactly as given here.

Must not appear: chat bubbles, avatars, robot or sparkle icons, microphone or paperclip icons, emoji,
logos, chips or pills of text, blue links, gradients, glow, any other person, any change to his face,
any text not written here.

## a-rest

The hero at rest. Under the lede, one row of three buttons, all the same height (about 48 px): the lime
filled "See my work" button, the outlined "Download CV" button as in the reference, and a third outlined
button "Ask me" with a tiny lime waveform burst (five short bars) before its label. The Ask button's
outline is the faint one, but its burst is lime, so it reads as the trace's invitation. On the phone,
the three buttons stack full width under the lede in the same order.

Built differently, at Berkay's call ("not three buttons"): the row keeps its two buttons, and under them a
quiet "Or ask me about my work" line, led by the same lime burst, opens the composer.

they are. The row of buttons is gone; in its place, the composer of the third attached image opens: a
generously rounded field (about 28 px corners) filled a touch lighter than the page (#17191C) with a
very thin #2A2D31 outline, about 760 px wide and 120 px tall on the desktop, one line tall and full width
on the phone, with a thin lime caret at its top left before the placeholder "Ask me anything about my
work" in dim monospace, and a round lime send button with a graphite up arrow at its bottom right. Its
bottom edge carries the trace: a lime line about 2 px thick runs round the bottom left corner a sixth of
