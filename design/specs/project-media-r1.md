# Project media r1: screenshots and videos on a project page

Status: a-reel approved and built (2026-10-06, `design/approved/project-media-reel.png`). The others
are deleted; git history keeps them.

The built project pages (`/projects/<id>`, from `pages-r1.md` b-project) show a project's media badly:
the cover screenshot sits small under the lede with the browser chrome still on it, and "Screens" is a
two-column grid of thumbnails inside the text column, with captions drifting below. Videos look the
same as stills. Six projects have media (Stats for Spotify has seven screenshots, VoxelCraft three
gameplay videos, Fractured Crown a cover video, Alertz and LithePlan a few screenshots). This round
mocks three ways to show them, desktop and phone on one board, on `/projects/statsforspotify`.

References attached with -i, in this order: a full-page screenshot of the current page
(`design/screens/before/project-media.1440.full.png`, for its content only, not its layout); the
approved landing hero (`design/approved/landing-desktop.png`) for colours, type, header and the trace;
two of the project's own screenshots (`public/images/projects/statsforspotify-overview.png`,
`public/images/projects/statsforspotify-top-artists.png`) to show as the media.

## Shared

A clean presentation board, 1536x1024, flat deep graphite #0A0B0C. On the left, about 1080 px wide, a
flat screenshot crop of a desktop web page, starting with its header (no browser chrome around it). On
the right, one iPhone-style screen (390x844 proportions, thin dark bezel, rounded corners), straight on,
no perspective, no hands, showing the same page on a phone. Even gaps, nothing else on the board, no
watermark.

The website is Berkay Orhan's portfolio in the look of the second attached image. Header as there:
"Berkay Orhan" in the grotesk at the left; "Work", "Research", "Experience", "About" and "EN / SV" in
small monospace; a lime "Contact" button with graphite text at the right. On the phone the header is
"Berkay Orhan", the lime "Contact" button and a two-line menu mark.

The page is the project page for Stats for Spotify, a music analytics web app. Its top, as in the first
attached image: "← All projects" in dim monospace, a small lime monospace index "PROJECT / 2026", the
headline "Stats for Spotify" in the grotesk with a dim monospace subtitle "Music Analytics Platform",
one lede sentence, and at the right a facts column of plain label and value pairs in monospace with
the links "Live site ↗" and "Source ↗" underlined. Lower down the sections run down the left, each
with a small lime waveform burst on the trace and a dim number ("01 / Overview").

The trace: one thin lime line, about 2 px, runs down the far left of the page (about 2 percent in on
desktop, 20 px in on the phone), like an oscilloscope trace: flat where nothing happens, a short
vertical waveform burst where a section starts, small lime ticks where it passes each item of a list.
Information hangs off it with thin faint leader lines.

The media are the third and fourth attached images: dark-mode screenshots of the app, near-black with
small green accents and album art. Show them as they are, cropped to the app itself with no browser
tabs or address bar, flat, unframed, with square corners, no shadow, no device mockup, no border. They
keep their own colours; everything around them stays graphite, off-white and lime. A caption is one
line of dim monospace with a dim number in front ("01  Dashboard overview with top artists, tracks,
albums and fun recaps").

Palette: background deep graphite #0F1012 with a very subtle grain, text off-white #ECEAE4, dim text
#8A8C90, faint #2A2D31 for rules and guide lines, one accent acid lime #C8F542. No other hues outside
the screenshots, no gradients, no glow, no gloss, no shadows, no 3D. Type: headlines in a geometric
grotesk like Space Grotesk 600 with tight tracking; everything else in a monospace like JetBrains Mono.
All text in English, crisp and legible.

Composition: editorial and asymmetric, big contrast in scale, lots of empty graphite. Information is
drawn or set as type, never boxed.

Must not appear: cards, boxes or panels with borders or fills, browser chrome or window frames around
screenshots, laptop or phone mockups inside the page, drop shadows, rounded image corners, tag pills or
chips, icons (no play triangles, no arrows in circles), emoji, logos, blue links, coloured buttons other
than the one lime button style, gradients, any person.

## a-reel

The desktop crop shows the page from the lede down. Under the lede and facts column, the cover
screenshot runs wide: from the text column's left edge to the right edge of the page, about 1000 px
wide, large enough to read the app's lists. Its top edge sits on a thin faint rule that branches off
the trace with a small lime tick, and a dim caption line sits under it at the left. Lower, the section
"06 / Screens" starts with a lime burst on the trace, and its screenshots run as a horizontal reel
across the page: three large screenshots side by side at the same height (about 360 px tall), the
third cut off by the right edge of the page to show the reel continues sideways. Above the reel a thin
faint rule runs left to right with a small lime tick over each screenshot, like stations on a line;
the first tick is lime and filled, the others dim. Under each screenshot its numbered caption. At the
right end of the rule, dim monospace "01 / 07" and underlined "Next →".

On the phone: the cover screenshot runs full width edge to edge under the lede, caption under it.
The screens reel is a sideways swipe: one screenshot nearly full width with the edge of the next
showing at the right, numbered caption under it, and "01 / 07" in dim monospace under that.

## b-beside

The desktop crop shows the page from "01 / Overview" down; the cover screenshot is not repeated as a
lone block. Each section is a two-column spread: the section's text on the left, about 40 percent of
the width, and on the right a large screenshot that belongs to that section, about 560 px wide, top
aligned with the section's number. "01 / Overview" pairs with the dashboard overview screenshot,
"02 / Key features" with the top artists grid screenshot, its dash list on the left. Each screenshot
has its numbered caption under it, right aligned with the image. A thin faint leader line runs from
the section's burst on the trace, under the text, to the screenshot's top left corner, so each image
hangs off the trace like everything else. Rows of sections without a screenshot keep the text at the
left and leave the right empty.

On the phone: each section is its number and title, then the screenshot full width edge to edge with
its caption, then the text under it.

## c-sheet

screenshot runs wide, about 1000 px, from the text column's left edge to the right edge of the page,
with its caption under it at the left. Then "06 / Screens" with a lime burst on the trace, and under

edges lining up with the large one's top and bottom. Below them a row of three equal smaller
screenshots. No gaps larger than 24 px between images. Captions are not under each image: they sit as
a numbered list in dim monospace at the left of the sheet, "01" to "07", one line each, with the
hovered one ("02  Top Artists grid view with artist cards") in off-white and a thin faint leader line
from it to its screenshot, whose number shows small in lime at its top left corner.

On the phone: the cover full width edge to edge with its caption, then the screens stacked one under
the other at full width, each with its numbered caption under it, the first two visible.
