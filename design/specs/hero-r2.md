# Hero r2: three directions of its own

Round 1 reskinned Genomlyst (its paper, palette, fonts and character style) and was rejected for it.
Round 2 takes only Genomlyst's method: one spec per round rendered by Codex, a calm page with one accent
and one human moment. Palette, type, medium and layout are new in every variant, and no Genomlyst image
is attached.

Reference, attached with -i: `public/images/profile.jpg` (the only one).

After review (2026-10-05): e-console kept. Berkay's face and body are never generated; the real photo is
cut out, made graphite duotone and faded below the neck, then composited (`e3-photo`, built from the
`e2-clean` edit with ImageMagick). The contextless fact lines are dropped.

## Shared

The first screen of Berkay Orhan's personal portfolio website. He is an AI engineer at Ericsson R&D in
Linköping, Sweden, finishing a master's in machine learning. The page should feel sharp, current and
confident, like the site of an engineer who ships.

Render: one landscape image, 1536x1024, a flat desktop website screenshot of the first screen (1440x1024
viewport). No device frame, no perspective, no browser chrome. All text in English, spelled exactly as
written here, crisp and legible. Generous empty space.

The attached photo is the person. Keep what makes him recognisable: dark swept-back hair, black
rectangular glasses, a short full dark beard. Do not show the suit, vest or tie; he wears what the
variant says.

Copy, used exactly as written wherever the variant places it:
- Name: "Berkay Orhan"
- Nav: "Work", "Research", "Experience", "About", "EN / SV", and a button "Contact"
- Overline: "AI ENGINEER · LINKÖPING"
- Headline, two lines: "I build AI that" / "works outside the demo."
- Lede: "Agentic retrieval, voice pipelines and the web apps around them. Now in R&D at Ericsson."
- Primary button "See my work", secondary link "Download CV"
- Facts: "11,366 test cases" (thesis, Ericsson), "first token < 2 s" (FastTalk), "since Jun 2026"
  (Ericsson R&D)

Must not appear: paper textures, linen, parchment, hand-drawn sketch or charcoal or stippled
illustration, serif display type, terracotta, butter yellow, stamps, paper chips, particles,
starfields, code rain, robots, brains, circuit boards, glowing networks, stock-photo people, emoji, any
text not written here.

## d-grid

Swiss typographic. Pure white #FFFFFF background, black #0A0A0A type, grey #8C8C8C for fine print, and
one accent, ultramarine #1F3DFF, used only for the overline, the primary button and one line in the chart.
Type: a tight neo-grotesk like Neue Haas Grotesk or Inter Display, the headline huge (about 110 px,
weight 700, tracking -0.04em) spanning two thirds of the width; numbers in a monospace like JetBrains
Mono. A visible 12-column grid with hairline rules. Header: "Berkay Orhan" left in the grotesk, nav right,
"Contact" as a black square-cornered button. Top left under the header: overline, then the headline,
then the lede in a narrow column. Right third: a tall black-and-white high-contrast halftone photo
portrait of him (from the attached photo, crew-neck black t-shirt), cropped at the shoulders, with a thin
ultramarine latency line chart crossing in front of its lower edge. Bottom band across the full width,
above a hairline rule: the three facts as three grid cells, each a big mono number and a small grey
label. Primary button ultramarine square-cornered "See my work", "Download CV" as an underlined link.

bloom.
