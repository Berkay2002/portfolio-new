# Stack r4: "how I build", patent plates

In round 3 (`stack-r3.md`) Berkay liked b-patent for its plates: hatched edges, dashed alignment lines,
leader lines. He did not like the drawings on the plates (chat bubbles, node graph, token bars, dot
scatter, cubes), which read as generic AI art. This round keeps the patent plates and tries three new
sets of drawings. References attached with -i: the round 3 patent board
(`design/mockups/stack-r3-b-patent.png`) first, for the plates, the layout and the labels; then the
approved hero (`design/approved/landing-desktop.png`) for colours and type.

## Shared

A clean presentation board, 1536x1024, flat deep graphite #0A0B0C. On the left, about 1080 px wide, a
flat screenshot crop of one part of a desktop website (no browser chrome, no header). On the right,
one iPhone-style screen (390x844 proportions, thin dark bezel, rounded corners), straight on, no
perspective, no hands, showing the same part of the same website on a phone. Even gaps, nothing else
on the board, no watermark.

Keep the first attached image's composition and plate style exactly: "05 / ABOUT" and the caption
"how I build" at the top left; five thin plates in oblique projection stacked one above the other with
even gaps, interface on top and infra at the bottom; hatching on each plate's visible front edge;
dashed vertical alignment lines between the plate corners; each layer's name and tools at the end of a
thin leader line with a small open circle where it touches its plate; "Download CV →" with a thin lime
underline at the bottom left. Everything in fine off-white ink line on graphite, like an engineering
patent sheet. Only the drawings on the plates' top faces change, as described in the variant.

The drawings on the plates are fine line work in off-white and grey, drawn as part of the patent
sheet with the same pen, flat on the plate and foreshortened with it. They should look drawn by an
engineer, not like icons, UI mockups or illustrations. Each plate has exactly one small lime element;
nothing else is lime except the CV underline and the index. No readable words inside any drawing.

Palette: background deep graphite #0F1012 with a very subtle grain, text off-white #ECEAE4, dim text
#8A8C90, greys for line work, one accent acid lime #C8F542. No other hues, no glow, no gloss, no
shading, no 3D rendering: everything is line drawing.

Type: monospace like JetBrains Mono. Layer names off-white about 15 px, tools dim about 13 px. The five
layers, in this order, with their tools, spelled exactly: "interface" / "Next.js · React"; "agents" /
"LangGraph · PydanticAI"; "models" / "vLLM · PyTorch"; "data" / "PostgreSQL · Qdrant · Neo4j";
"infra" / "Docker · Kubernetes". All text in English, crisp and legible.

On the phone the plates are narrower (about 55 percent of the screen width) so the labels to their
right have room: the tools may wrap to a second line, but only between whole tool names, never inside
one. Margins 24 px.

Must not appear: chat bubbles, message threads, node-and-arrow graphs, bar charts, dot scatters,
cubes, cards, boxes or panels holding text, tables, tag pills, skill bars, logos of the tools, icons,
robots, brains, glowing neural networks, circuit boards, particles, code rain, emoji, any person.

## a-surfaces

Each plate is machined with a surface pattern that stands for its layer, like different finishes on
precision parts. interface: a fine layout grid, twelve thin columns and a few rows, with three or four
regions of the grid filled with fine diagonal hatching, like a page layout; the lime element is one
thin rule marking a focused field. agents: a single continuous groove routed across the plate that
runs forward, turns back on itself in one clean loop and carries on, with small round pads where it
pauses; the lime element is one pad. models: a dense square matrix of tiny cells, most empty, many
lightly hatched in a varied pattern, like a weight matrix; the lime element is one filled cell.
data: fine topographic contour lines, like an embedding landscape, with a few closed rings around
low points; the lime element is a small dot in the deepest ring. infra: a row of identical narrow
slots cut through the plate at even spacing, like a rack; the lime element is one slot outline.

## b-signal

Each plate shows what that layer does to a signal passing through the stack, drawn as fine line work
on the plate. interface: a short irregular input waveform entering from the plate's left edge, like
someone typing or speaking. agents: the waveform splits into three thin paths across the plate, and
one of them curves back to the start in a loop before joining the others. models: the smooth waveform
becomes a row of evenly spaced short discrete ticks, like tokens, all grey except the last one in lime.
data: the ticks reach out with thin straight lines to a few small points spread across the plate,
like lookups, with fine contour rings around them. infra: the signal is copied onto five identical
thin parallel lanes running across the plate, like replicas, one lane dashed. One thin dashed lime
line drops vertically through all five plates where the signal passes, with a small lime dot where it
pierces each plate; in this variant that line is each plate's one lime element.

## c-schematic

Each plate carries a small engineering schematic of its layer, in the visual language of real patent
figures. Next to each plate's leader circle sits a small reference numeral, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 from top
to bottom, in dim monospace, and the same numeral sits before the layer name in the label ("10
interface"). interface: a component tree drawn as nested rounded outlines, one large outline holding
two smaller ones, one of which holds three tiny ones; the lime element is the smallest outline.
agents: a patent flowchart without words, three small empty outlines and one diamond decision shape
in a row joined by fine lines, with a return line from the diamond back to the first outline; the lime
element is the diamond. models: a short column of four identical repeated layers drawn as thin flat
slabs stacked on the plate, with fine lines fanning between a row of small dots above and below, like
attention; the lime element is one dot. data: three storage forms side by side, a small ruled grid of
rows, a small field of points with one ring, and a small triangle of linked points; the lime element
is the ring. infra: one large rounded outline, a cluster, holding three medium outlines, nodes, each
holding two small circles, pods; the lime element is one pod.
