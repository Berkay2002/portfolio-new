# Hero r1: common to all variants

The first screen of Berkay Orhan's personal portfolio. He is an AI engineer at Ericsson R&D in
Linköping, Sweden, finishing a master's in machine learning. The page should feel calm and competent,
like a well-made printed document, with one warm human moment: a hand-drawn character of him.

Render: one landscape image, 1536x1024, a flat desktop website screenshot of the first screen
(1440x1024 viewport). No device frame, no perspective, no browser chrome. All text in English, spelled
exactly as written below, crisp and legible. At least 40 % of the frame is calm, empty paper.

## References (attached with -i, in this order)

1. The Genomlyst landing hero: the paper, the palette, the type, the depth and the overall restraint to
   match. Do NOT copy its words, its logo, its layout or its scene.
2. The Genomlyst character: the drawing style to match exactly (grainy charcoal outline, stippled dot
   shading, fills with print texture, a large head about a quarter of the body height, short compact
   limbs). Do NOT draw this woman.
3. A photo of Berkay: the person to draw, in the style of reference 2. Keep what makes him recognisable:
   dark swept-back hair, black rectangular glasses, a short full dark beard. Friendly, relaxed expression.
   He wears a chunky butter-yellow knit sweater over a white collar, not the suit from the photo.

## Palette

- Paper background `#F3EEE5` with a fine linen grain over everything.
- Sheet `#FBF8F2` for every piece of paper on the page (sheets, chips).
- Ink `#242424` for the wordmark, headline, body, nav, line work and chip text.
- Muted ink `#5F5A53` for fine print.
- Black `#111111` for the pill buttons.
- Terracotta `#C8643B` as the only accent: the overline, the hand underline, the stamp, small sparks.
  Scarce: one accent moment per region.
- Butter `#F7C977` for the sweater and at most one highlight marker.
- No other hues. No blue, no pink, no green. No gradients except soft shadows, no glow, no glossy 3D.

## Type

- Wordmark and headline: a soft, heavy variable serif like Fraunces (weight 800, slightly soft
  terminals), tight tracking.
- Lede, nav and body: a readable text serif like Source Serif.
- Overline and buttons: a clean grotesk like Inter, overline uppercase with wide tracking.
- Numbers, dates and chip values: a monospace like IBM Plex Mono.

## Header (all variants)

About 85 px tall, outer margins about 73 px. Left: the wordmark "Berkay Orhan" in the heavy serif, ink,
with a small terracotta mark of three short strokes after it. Right, in the text serif: "Work",
"Research", "Experience", "About", a small "EN / SV" in muted ink, and a black pill button "Contact" with
light text.

## Text column (variants a and c; variant b sets the same words differently)

Left column about 560 px wide, its top at about a quarter of the viewport height.

- Overline, terracotta, uppercase, wide tracking: "AI ENGINEER · LINKÖPING"
- Headline, two lines, about 69 px: "I build AI that" / "works outside the demo." A hand-drawn terracotta
  underline sits under the whole phrase "outside the demo".
- Lede, two lines, about 22 px: "Agentic retrieval, voice pipelines and the web apps around them. Now in
  R&D at Ericsson."
- Action row: a black pill "See my work" with light text, and next to it the text link "Download CV" in
  ink with a thin underline.
- Fine print, muted, about 17 px: "Master's in machine learning, Linköping University."

## Depth

- One warm light from the upper left. Paper casts soft shadows down and to the right; paper edges show
  thickness and a slight curl.
- Behind the scene: one loose page floating at an angle upper right, softly out of focus.
- In front: one loose page close to the camera bottom right, cropped by the frame and clearly blurred.
- Blurred pages never cover text, buttons or the header.

## Bottom

Bottom centre, small text serif "Selected work" with a thin arrow down. Nothing else at the bottom.

## Must not appear

Particles, starfields, neon, code rain, robots, brains, circuit boards, glowing networks, laptops
showing readable code, stock-photo people, emoji, any text not listed here.
