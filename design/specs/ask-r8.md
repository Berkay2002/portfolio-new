# Ask r8: the hero becomes the conversation

Status: a-bubbles picked and built with ask-r9; b-bursts rejected (2026-10-06).

Berkay on the built `ask-r7` hero: once a question is sent, the headline and the lede go, the composer
docks at the bottom of the hero just above the signal, and the messages lay out above it as a
conversation. Two takes on the messages. References attached with -i, in this order: the approved board
`design/approved/landing-hero-ask.png` (keep the portrait exactly as it is there; it is a real photo and
must not be redrawn).

## Shared

A clean presentation board, 1536x1024, flat deep graphite #0A0B0C. On the left, about 1080 px wide, a
flat screenshot of the first screen of a desktop website, starting with its header (no browser chrome).
On the right, one iPhone-style screen (390x844 proportions, thin dark bezel, rounded corners), straight
on, no perspective, no hands, showing the first screen on a phone. Even gaps, nothing else on the board,
no watermark.

The website is Berkay Orhan's portfolio in the look of the attached image: the same header ("Berkay
Orhan" left; "Work", "Research", "Experience", "About", "Ask", "EN / SV" in small monospace; a lime
"Contact" button), the same rounded composer with the lime trace on its bottom left edge breaking into a
short waveform burst and the round lime send button with a graphite up arrow. The headline and the lede
are gone: the visitor has already asked two questions, so the hero is now a conversation.

Desktop: the portrait stays on the right as in the reference, a little dimmer. The composer is docked
low in the left two thirds of the hero, about 760 px wide and one line tall (about 60 px), empty with the
placeholder "Ask a follow-up" in dim monospace. Just under it a thin lime waveform runs across the page,
flat with one burst, as in the reference's lower edge. Above the composer, in the space where the
headline was, the conversation reads top to bottom, newest just above the composer, and a small dim
monospace "New question" link sits at the top left of it. Phone: the portrait is gone; the conversation
fills the screen under the header and the composer is docked at the bottom above the thin waveform.

The conversation, exactly this text:
1. Visitor: "Which projects use LangGraph?"
2. Answer: "Two of them. The Municipality Chatbot uses LangGraph to orchestrate its answers, and SynGraph
   uses it to run a supervisor and its worker agents." with "Municipality Chatbot" and "SynGraph" lit lime.
3. Visitor: "What does SynGraph search?"
4. Answer: "The web and academic papers. Its worker agents read sources in parallel and the supervisor
   writes a cited report." with "SynGraph" lit lime.

Palette: background deep graphite #0F1012, text off-white #ECEAE4, dim text #8A8C90, faint #2A2D31, one
accent acid lime #C8F542. No other hues, no gradients, no glow, no shadows, no 3D. Type: answers in a
geometric grotesk like Space Grotesk; the visitor's questions, labels and the placeholder in a monospace
like JetBrains Mono. All text in English, crisp and legible, exactly as given here.

Must not appear: avatars, robot or sparkle icons, microphone or paperclip icons, emoji, logos,
timestamps, chips or pills of text, blue links, gradients, glow, any other person, any change to his
face, any text not written here.

## a-bubbles

The familiar chat layout: each visitor question is right-aligned in a softly rounded bubble filled a touch
lighter than the page (#17191C) with a very thin #2A2D31 outline, monospace at about 15 px. Each answer
is left-aligned with no bubble, off-white grotesk at about 20 px on desktop and 16 px on the phone, with
generous space between turns.

## b-bursts

The trace carries the answers: a thin lime vertical line runs down the left edge of the conversation and
joins the composer's lime corner below. Each answer hangs off that line on a small lime waveform burst,
off-white grotesk at about 20 px on desktop and 16 px on the phone, with no bubble. Each visitor question
sits above its answer, left-aligned with the answers, in dim monospace at about 14 px, with no bubble.
