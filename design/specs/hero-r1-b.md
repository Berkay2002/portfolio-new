# Hero r1-b: "The paper"

Read `hero-r1-common.md` first; everything there applies except the text column, which this variant
sets as a printed title page. References: the three listed there.

Concept: Berkay writes research and ships it, so the first screen is the title page of his own paper,
lying on the linen.

## Layout

- The header as in the common spec.
- One large sheet (`#FBF8F2`), about 820 x 720 px, centred slightly left, rotated about -1.5 degrees,
  with a soft long shadow and a slight curl at the bottom right corner. A staple top left.
- On the sheet, centred like an academic title page:
  - Small terracotta overline, uppercase, wide tracking: "AI ENGINEER · LINKÖPING"
  - Title in the heavy serif, two lines, about 60 px: "I build AI that" / "works outside the demo."
    with the hand-drawn terracotta underline under "outside the demo".
  - Author line in the text serif: "Berkay Orhan"
  - Affiliation line in the monospace, muted: "Ericsson R&D · Linköping University"
  - A short rule, then "Abstract" in small bold serif, then the lede as the abstract, left-aligned in a
    narrow column: "Agentic retrieval, voice pipelines and the web apps around them. Now in R&D at
    Ericsson."
  - At the bottom of the sheet: the black pill "See my work" and the text link "Download CV".
- In the page margin right of the sheet, handwritten in terracotta with a thin hand-drawn arrow pointing
  at the abstract: "see FastTalk". This is the region's one accent besides the overline and underline.
- Berkay, drawn small (about 300 px tall), standing at the sheet's lower right corner, leaning on it with
  one hand, holding a pencil in the other, looking at the viewer with a small smile. Full body, feet on
  the paper, a short soft shadow under him.
- Fine print below the sheet, muted: "Master's in machine learning, Linköping University."
- No stamp and no chips in this variant.
