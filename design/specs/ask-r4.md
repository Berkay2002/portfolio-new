## Shared

A clean presentation board, 1536x1024, flat deep graphite #0A0B0C. On the left, about 1080 px wide, a
flat screenshot of the first screen of a desktop website, starting with its header (no browser chrome).
On the right, one iPhone-style screen (390x844 proportions, thin dark bezel, rounded corners), straight
on, no perspective, no hands, showing the first screen on a phone. Even gaps, nothing else on the board,
no watermark.

The website is Berkay Orhan's portfolio in the look of the first attached image: the same header
("Berkay Orhan" left; "Work", "Research", "Experience", "About", "EN / SV" in small monospace; a lime
"Contact" button) and the same portrait of him in the black hoodie fading into the graphite. The hero
holds only three things and nothing else: the headline "I build AI that" / "works outside the demo.",
under it one lede line in dim monospace, "Agentic retrieval, voice pipelines and the web apps around
them. Now a software developer at Ericsson.", and the composer. No overline, no "See my work", no
"Download CV", no other text in the hero.

The composer is the point of the screen: a place to type a question about Berkay's work, like the
message composer of a modern chat app, generous and inviting, clearly the thing to use. Its placeholder
is "Ask me anything about my work". Its send key is a lime square with a graphite arrow drawn in two
strokes.

On the desktop nobody has asked yet: the composer is focused and empty, with a lime caret before the
placeholder, and three example questions in small dim monospace as the variant places them: "Which
projects use LangGraph?", "What does he do at Ericsson?", "Anything with voice?". On the phone the
visitor has asked "Which projects use LangGraph?" and the answer came back under the composer, in the
grotesk: "Two of them. The Municipality Chatbot uses LangGraph to orchestrate its answers, and SynGraph
uses it to run a supervisor and its worker agents.", with "Municipality Chatbot" and "SynGraph" lit lime,
then a tiny dim monospace status line with a small lime dot: "answers from a free model on my home
server".

Palette: background deep graphite #0F1012, text off-white #ECEAE4, dim text #8A8C90, faint #2A2D31 for
rules and outlines, one accent acid lime #C8F542. No other hues, no gradients, no glow, no shadows, no
3D. Type: headlines in a geometric grotesk like Space Grotesk 600 with tight tracking; everything else in
a monospace like JetBrains Mono. All text in English, crisp and legible, exactly as given here.

Must not appear: chat bubbles, avatars, robot or sparkle icons, emoji, logos of companies or models,
tag pills or chips, blue links, gradients, glow, any other person, any change to his face.
