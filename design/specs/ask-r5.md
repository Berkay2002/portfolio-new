# Ask r5: a rounded composer with a touch of the trace

Status: superseded by ask-r7 (2026-10-06).

Berkay on `ask-r4`: no example questions, no "answers from a free model" line (too much text, too much
height), and the composer rounded like ChatGPT's and Claude's, with a hint of the trace. The hero keeps
only the headline, the lede and the composer. The desktop shows the composer waiting, the phone after a
question was answered.

References attached with -i, in this order: the approved landing hero (`design/approved/landing-desktop.png`)
and the approved phone boards (`design/approved/landing-mobile-1.png`, the first phone only). Keep the
portrait exactly as it is there; it is a real photo and must not be redrawn.

## Shared

A clean presentation board, 1536x1024, flat deep graphite #0A0B0C. On the left, about 1080 px wide, a
flat screenshot of the first screen of a desktop website, starting with its header (no browser chrome).
On the right, one iPhone-style screen (390x844 proportions, thin dark bezel, rounded corners), straight
on, no perspective, no hands, showing the first screen on a phone. Even gaps, nothing else on the board,
no watermark.

The website is Berkay Orhan's portfolio in the look of the first attached image: the same header
("Berkay Orhan" left; "Work", "Research", "Experience", "About", "EN / SV" in small monospace; a lime
"Contact" button) and the same portrait of him in the black hoodie fading into the graphite. The hero
holds only three things: the headline "I build AI that" / "works outside the demo.", under it one lede
line in dim monospace, "Agentic retrieval, voice pipelines and the web apps around them. Now a software
developer at Ericsson.", and the composer. Nothing else: no overline, no buttons, no example questions,
no status line, no hints, no labels.

The composer is shaped like the message composer of ChatGPT or Claude: a generously rounded field
(corner radius about 26 px), about 720 px wide and 64 px tall, filled a touch lighter than the page
(#17191C) with a very thin #2A2D31 outline, the placeholder "Ask me anything about my work" in dim
grotesk at about 18 px, and at its right end a round lime send button (about 40 px) with a graphite
up arrow drawn in two strokes. Calm, soft and simple. On the desktop it is empty and focused. On the
phone it is full width, and the visitor has asked "Which projects use LangGraph?": the question stays in
the composer and under it, with no label, the answer in off-white grotesk at about 16 px: "Two of them.
The Municipality Chatbot uses LangGraph to orchestrate its answers, and SynGraph uses it to run a
supervisor and its worker agents.", with "Municipality Chatbot" and "SynGraph" lit lime.

The trace is the site's motif: one thin lime line, about 2 px, like an oscilloscope trace, flat where
nothing happens and a short vertical waveform burst where something does. Here it appears only as the
variant says, as a hint, never loud.

Palette: background deep graphite #0F1012, text off-white #ECEAE4, dim text #8A8C90, faint #2A2D31, one
accent acid lime #C8F542. No other hues, no gradients, no glow, no shadows, no 3D. Type: headlines in a
geometric grotesk like Space Grotesk 600 with tight tracking; the lede in a monospace like JetBrains
Mono. All text in English, crisp and legible, exactly as given here.

Must not appear: chat bubbles, avatars, robot or sparkle icons, microphone or paperclip icons, emoji,
logos, chips or pills of text, blue links, gradients, glow, any other person, any change to his face,
any text not written here.

## a-caret

The hero's waveform runs across under the hero as in the reference, and its flat line runs into the
composer from the left: it enters at the composer's left end and ends as the lime text caret before the
placeholder. Nothing else lime in the composer but the caret and the send button.

## b-pulse

The composer sits a little above the hero's waveform, which runs across under it as in the reference.
Inside the composer, just left of the send button, a tiny lime waveform burst about 40 px wide and 16 px
tall, like a level meter, quiet now. On the phone, where the answer has come, that small burst is taller,
as if it just spoke.

## c-edge

No waveform across the page. The composer's thin outline is faint all round except along its bottom
edge, where a lime line about 2 px thick runs from the left corner a third of the way along and breaks
into a short waveform burst, then fades back to faint, like the trace passing along the composer.

## d-lane

The trace runs down the far left of the page, about 2 percent in, thin and lime, as on the site's other
pages. Level with the composer it bends right in a soft curve and runs flat into the composer's left
end, with a small lime dot where it meets the outline. On the phone it runs down the left lane, 20 px
in, and bends into the composer the same way.
