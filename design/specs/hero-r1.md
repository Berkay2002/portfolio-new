# Hero r1: three first-screen concepts and an accent variant

Round 1 of the portfolio hero. Each render passes the `## Shared` section followed by one variant
section verbatim as the image prompt (`design/scripts/render.ps1`). `a2-blue` is an edit of the
`a-workbench` render and passes its own section alone.

References, attached with -i in this order (a, b and c):
1. Genomlyst `design/approved/landing-desktop.png`
2. Genomlyst `design/assets/character-raw.png`
3. `public/images/profile.jpg`

## Shared

The first screen of Berkay Orhan's personal portfolio website. He is an AI engineer at Ericsson R&D in
Linköping, Sweden, finishing a master's in machine learning. The page feels calm and competent, like a
well-made printed document, with one warm human moment: a hand-drawn character of him.

Render: one landscape image, 1536x1024, a flat desktop website screenshot of the first screen (1440x1024
viewport). No device frame, no perspective, no browser chrome. All text in English, spelled exactly as
written here, crisp and legible. At least 40 % of the frame is calm, empty paper.

The attached images, in order:
1. A website hero to match for paper, palette, type, depth and restraint. Do NOT copy its words, its
   logo, its layout or its scene.
2. A character drawing whose style to match exactly: grainy charcoal outline, stippled dot shading,
   fills with print texture, a large head about a quarter of the body height, short compact limbs. Do
   NOT draw this woman.
3. A photo of the person to draw, in the style of image 2. Keep what makes him recognisable: dark
   swept-back hair, black rectangular glasses, a short full dark beard. A friendly, relaxed expression.
   He wears a chunky butter-yellow knit sweater over a white collar, not the suit from the photo.

Palette: paper background #F3EEE5 with a fine linen grain over everything. Sheet #FBF8F2 for every piece
of paper on the page. Ink #242424 for the wordmark, headline, body, nav, line work and chip text. Muted
ink #5F5A53 for fine print. Black #111111 for the pill buttons. Terracotta #C8643B as the only accent:
the overline, the hand underline, the stamp, small sparks; one accent moment per region. Butter #F7C977
for the sweater and at most one highlight. No other hues: no blue, no pink, no green. No gradients
except soft shadows, no glow, no glossy 3D.

Type: wordmark and headline in a soft, heavy variable serif like Fraunces (weight 800, slightly soft
terminals), tight tracking. Lede, nav and body in a readable text serif like Source Serif. Overline and
buttons in a clean grotesk like Inter, the overline uppercase with wide tracking. Numbers, dates and chip
values in a monospace like IBM Plex Mono.

Header, about 85 px tall, outer margins about 73 px. Left: the wordmark "Berkay Orhan" in the heavy
serif, ink, with a small terracotta mark of three short strokes after it. Right, in the text serif:
"Work", "Research", "Experience", "About", a small "EN / SV" in muted ink, and a black pill button
"Contact" with light text.

Text column, unless the variant says otherwise: left, about 560 px wide, its top at about a quarter of
the viewport height.
- Overline, terracotta, uppercase, wide tracking: "AI ENGINEER · LINKÖPING"
- Headline, two lines, about 69 px: "I build AI that" / "works outside the demo." A hand-drawn terracotta
  underline sits under the whole phrase "outside the demo".
- Lede, two lines, about 22 px: "Agentic retrieval, voice pipelines and the web apps around them. Now in
  R&D at Ericsson."
- Action row: a black pill "See my work" with light text, and next to it the text link "Download CV" in
  ink with a thin underline.
- Fine print, muted, about 17 px: "Master's in machine learning, Linköping University."

Depth: one warm light from the upper left. Paper casts soft shadows down and to the right; paper edges
show thickness and a slight curl. Behind the scene, one loose page floats at an angle upper right,
softly out of focus. In front, one loose page close to the camera bottom right, cropped by the frame and
clearly blurred. Blurred pages never cover text, buttons or the header.

Bottom centre: small text serif "Selected work" with a thin arrow down. Nothing else at the bottom.

Must not appear: particles, starfields, neon, code rain, robots, brains, circuit boards, glowing
networks, screens with readable code, stock-photo people, emoji, any text not written here.

## a-workbench

Concept: the work shown as printed paper. Berkay at his desk; one of his projects lies on it as a printed
sheet, and three facts lift off the page as small paper chips, caught mid-motion.

Scene right of centre, about 700 px wide including the chips, clear of the header:
- Berkay seen from the waist up behind a desk, one elbow on the desk and his chin on his hand, looking at
  the sheet with a small, pleased smile. A closed laptop with a plain lid lies at his other elbow.
- On the desk in front of him, slightly rotated toward the viewer, a printed sheet with a folded corner
  top right, monospace text exactly: "FASTTALK", "Voice pipeline", a dashed rule, "9 latency metrics ·
  3 services".
- A terracotta stamp pressed onto the sheet's top right corner, rotated about -8 degrees: a ring with the
  word "SHIPPED", with an ink texture. Two or three short terracotta spark strokes beside it.
- A few loose plain sheets under his arms with short grey lines for text, no readable words.
- Three small paper chips rising from the sheet into the empty paper above it, staggered, each with a
  soft shadow, a small dot left of the text (butter, ink, terracotta), label and value in the monospace:
  "Thesis · Ericsson" / "11,366 test cases"; "FastTalk" / "first token < 2 s"; "Ericsson R&D" /
  "since Jun 2026".

  with the hand-drawn terracotta underline under "outside the demo"; the author line "Berkay Orhan" in

  sheet the black pill "See my work" and the text link "Download CV".

## c-desk

Concept: a flat lay. The right half of the screen is Berkay's desk seen from straight above, his work
spread out on it, his hands and sleeves entering the frame.

- Seen from straight above, slight perspective only in the paper curl. Every object drawn in the
  character style (grainy charcoal outline, stippled shading), on the linen paper. About 720 px wide,
  clear of the header.
- His forearms in the butter-yellow knit sleeves enter from the bottom edge, hands resting on a plain
  laptop keyboard; the laptop screen is out of frame or shows only a soft grey waveform, no text.
- Around the laptop, slightly overlapping and rotated: a stapled thesis booklet with the cover title in
  the heavy serif exactly "When Agentic Workflows Help" and a small mono line "Master's thesis · Ericsson
  · 2026"; a notebook open on a pencil sketch of a small graph of nodes and arrows, no words; a printed
  sheet with a folded corner, monospace exactly "FASTTALK" / "Voice pipeline" and short grey lines below;
  a phone face up showing a simple voice waveform; a coffee cup seen from above, half full.
- Top right, a small paper photo print pinned at an angle with a strip of tape: his portrait drawn in the
  character style, head and shoulders, smiling.
- One terracotta accent: a hand-drawn circle around the thesis booklet's title.
- No chips and no stamp.

## a2-blue

Edit the attached image. Change ONLY the terracotta accent #C8643B to pen blue #2B4BA8: the overline,
the hand underline under "outside the demo", the small mark after the wordmark "Berkay Orhan", the
SHIPPED stamp, the spark strokes and the chip dot that is terracotta. Everything else stays exactly as it
is: layout, sizes, positions, the linen paper, the ink text, the butter sweater and butter chip dot, the
black pills, the blurred pages, the chips, the character.
