# Portrait r1: the hero photo

The hero portrait was cropped out of the approved mockup (`design/approved/landing-desktop.png`), which
gives only 616x560 px, and Berkay said it looks bad. This round renders the portrait on its own at full
size, from his real photo, so the hero can use it as an image and fade it into the page. It must stay a
photograph of him: no drawing, no stylised character, no invented body. References: 1 is his real photo
(`public/images/profile.png`), 2 is the approved hero for the hoodie, light and grade.

## Shared

A portrait photograph of the man in the first attached photo, for the hero of his personal website.
It is a real photograph, not an illustration, painting or 3D render: true skin texture, pores, stray
hairs, natural asymmetry. His face must be exactly the face in the first photo: the same eyes, nose,
mouth, jaw, ears, hairline, dark swept-back hair, black rectangular glasses and short full dark beard.
Do not beautify, slim, age or change him.

Render: one portrait image, 1024x1536, head and upper chest, his face in the upper half, looking
straight into the camera with a calm, neutral expression. He wears a plain dark charcoal hoodie, no
logo, no drawstrings showing text, no suit, vest or tie.

Light and colour: a dark studio. One soft key light from the front left, gentle falloff, the right side
of his face slightly in shadow. Background a flat deep graphite #0F1012 with no texture, no gradient
spots, no objects; the edges of the frame and everything below the chest fall off into the same
#0F1012 so the photo can blend into a page of that colour. A muted grade with a very slight cool green
cast in the shadows, like the second attached image. No glow, no rim light in colour, no lime, no text,
no watermark, no frame.
