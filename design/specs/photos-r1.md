# Photos r1: the photos beside "how I build"

On desktop the About section has the exploded stack on the left and four small grayscale photos in a
narrow column on the right. The column ends halfway down, sits still and leaves the right side empty
beside the lower plates. On the phone the same four photos are a 2x2 grid under "Download CV". Berkay
asked for something that makes the photos part of the section. This round tries three directions.
References attached with -i: the current About section on desktop (`design/screens/about.1440.full.png`)
first, then the same section on the phone (`design/screens/about.390.full.png`). Use the photos in
them (the temple, Mount Fuji, the lighthouse, the tram) as the photos; do not invent new ones.

## Shared

A clean presentation board, 1536x1024, flat deep graphite #0A0B0C. On the left, about 1080 px wide, a
flat screenshot crop of one part of a desktop website (no browser chrome, no header). On the right,
one iPhone-style screen (390x844 proportions, thin dark bezel, rounded corners), straight on, no
perspective, no hands, showing the same part of the same website on a phone. Even gaps, nothing else
on the board, no watermark.

The website part is the About section in the first attached image. Keep its left side exactly: the
thin lime vertical line at the far left, "05 / ABOUT", the large headline with "after the demo" in
lime, "how I build" and the exploded drawing of five thin patent-style plates with leader lines and
labels, "Download CV →" with a lime underline. Only the photos on the right change, as described in
the variant. On the phone the section is stacked: headline, the plates with labels to their right,
"Download CV →", then the photos, as in the second attached image.

The photos are the four in the attached images: a temple roof, Mount Fuji, a lighthouse on a pier, an
old tram. They stay real photographs, in grayscale, with the one in focus shown in colour. Small
monospace text beside them: "off the clock: photography, Japan and Portugal" in dim grey and "All 67
photos →" in off-white with a thin lime underline.

Palette: background deep graphite #0F1012 with a very subtle grain, text off-white #ECEAE4, dim text
#8A8C90, greys for line work, one accent acid lime #C8F542. No other hues except inside the one colour
photo, no glow, no gloss, no shadows, no 3D rendering. Type: monospace like JetBrains Mono for the
small text, the site's rounded grotesk for the headline. All text in English, crisp and legible.

Must not appear: cards, boxes or panels holding text, tables, tag pills, icons, logos, emoji, camera
illustrations, polaroid frames with handwriting, tape, pins, any person.

## a-filmstrip

The photos become a strip of 35mm film negatives developed as positives, running vertically down the
right side of the section for its whole height, beside the plates. Fine off-white line work draws the
film edges with small rectangular sprocket holes down both sides, and tiny dim frame numbers (14, 15,
15A, 16) printed along the edge like real film. The frames are the photos, in grayscale, about 230 px
wide on desktop. One frame, level with the middle of the plates, sits inside a thin lime bracket
marking it like a gate; that frame alone is in colour and has a small dim caption under it ("Japan").
The strip runs off the top and bottom of the crop, as if it keeps going. The caption and "All
67 photos →" sit at the bottom right under the strip. On the phone the strip runs horizontally across
the full width under "Download CV →", sprocket holes along its top and bottom edges, frames about 150
px wide, the middle frame in colour inside the lime bracket and the strip cut off at both screen edges.

## b-viewfinder

One photo at a time, large, in a camera viewfinder drawn in the same fine patent line style as the
plates. On desktop it sits in the right column level with the plates, about 360 px wide, 4:5: thin
off-white corner marks at the four corners, a small lime focus bracket on the subject, faint
rule-of-thirds lines, and under it one row of small dim monospace readout: "03 / 67" and "Japan".
The photo in the viewfinder is in colour. Under the readout, a single row of four tiny
grayscale thumbnails of the four photos, the current one underlined in lime. "off the clock:
photography, Japan and Portugal" and "All 67 photos →" below the thumbnails. On the phone the
viewfinder takes the full content width under "Download CV →", with the same corner marks, lime focus
bracket, readout and thumbnail row.

## c-prints

The photos are a pile of real prints that pulls apart the same way the plates do, so the right side
echoes the exploded stack. On desktop, beside the plates, four photographic prints with a thin
off-white border are fanned out down the right column: each one slightly rotated (between -4 and 5
degrees), overlapping the next by about a fifth, offset a little left and right, like prints dealt
onto a table. Thin dashed vertical alignment lines, like the plates', run behind them. Each print has
a small dim monospace place under its corner ("Japan", "Japan", "Portugal", "Portugal"). The top print is in
colour, the rest grayscale. On the phone the four prints are dealt in a loose overlapping row across
the full width under "Download CV →", each slightly rotated, the first in colour, with the places
under them.
